# Stage 8: pure or mixed? Shallow random circuits

Pure U|0..0> vs mixed U rho_0 U^dagger, U a random brickwork circuit of the given depth (CZ layers), fresh per state; 'haar' = stage 5's Haar-random states. 200 training and 400 test states per (n, depth); 3 seeds. The QNN is trained on Haar states only (2 qubits, fine-tuned at n) and applied unchanged to every depth. Classical: best of LR / RBF SVM / MLP chosen on the test set (flatters the classical side). c copies = c preparations (QNN: c/2 shots of two preparations; shadows: c snapshots).
